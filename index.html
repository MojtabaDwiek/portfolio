OUR STRAIGHT FORWARD PROCESS

  • STEP 1
    Scope & Agreement
    We begin by aligning on a detailed project plan and fixed price. To initiate work, a 30% deposit is paid, with the remaining 70% due upon your final approval at project completion.
  • STEP 2
    Design & Approval
    Our designers craft the user experience and visual identity, presenting iterations for your feedback. We refine the designs until you provide your formal sign-off, ensuring the vision is exactly right before we build.
  • STEP 3
    Development & Integration
    Our engineering team brings the approved designs to life. We build the core product with clean, scalable code, integrating all front-end and back-end systems into a fully functional application.
  • STEP 4
    Iteration & Refinement
    During the build, we accommodate necessary changes or new insights. This phase allows for collaborative refinements to design or functionality, ensuring the final product not only meets but exceeds expectations.
  • STEP 5
    Launch & Satisfaction
    We conduct final testing and prepare for handoff. Upon your final satisfaction and approval, we deliver the complete project, collect the remaining balance, and provide all assets—launching a product you’re proud to own.
